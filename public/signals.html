What each signal status means

Filtered — not qualified

A possible setup was found, but one or more model, quality, confluence or risk gates rejected it. No entry is tracked.

Waiting for entry

The setup qualified. ForexBot is waiting for price to reach the planned confirmation entry.

Entry triggered — active

Price reached the planned entry. ForexBot is now monitoring the stop, target and holding-time limit.

Expired — entry never triggered

The allowed entry window closed before price reached the planned entry, so the setup was discarded without a trade result.

Finished — result recorded

The setup completed. Its target, stop or time-limit outcome has been stored in the accuracy statistics.

Time limit reached — win / loss

Neither target nor stop was hit before the maximum holding period. ForexBot closes the evaluation at the final candle and marks it a win or loss after estimated costs.
